From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Diffusion is the net movement of anything from a region of higher concentration to a region of lower concentration. A reading mode must open on the article title, not on the titlebar toggle or the language switcher that Vector places inside the same main element.

This fixture keeps the Vector landmarks that previously leaked into the Markdown — the empty titlebar contents control, the interlanguage portlet, and the tools strips — while the body holds only original prose long enough to clear the extract length floor.

History

Early quantitative work treated diffusion as a continuous flux proportional to a concentration gradient. The point of this section is not encyclopedia coverage; it is a real heading the sidebar TOC can point at without the sidebar being part of the capture root.

Additional sentences exist so paragraph density and visible text both look like an article rather than a chrome shell with a teaser. The scorer should pick main and the furniture list should drop the interface controls listed above.

Models

Simple models describe a diffusion flux along a single axis. More elaborate models couple several species or a temperature field; the fixture only needs the heading id and a short paragraph of original text under the content container.
