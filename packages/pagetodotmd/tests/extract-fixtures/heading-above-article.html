How sectioning splits a title from its body

The paragraphs live inside the article element, which is what a length scorer will prefer.

The document title sits one level up as a sibling heading, and a capture of the article alone would drop it.

Lifting only the nearest preceding h1 under the same sectioning parent keeps the title without dragging in the site header logo and menus.

More body text keeps this root well above any soft length floor the consumer might set for a refusal.
